Fill every `{{...}}`, delete what does not apply, and send. The converter is a cheaper model: be literal.

You are converting one Thai PDF into a DOCX and an XLSX. Work only inside `{{LANE}}`.

## Boundaries
- Read-only: `{{LANE}}/sources/`. Do not modify it. Do not run git commit/push.
- Tools available: PyMuPDF (fitz), python-docx, openpyxl, the scripts in `~/.codex/skills/thai-pdf-to-docx/scripts/`.
  No OCR engine: scanned pages are read by looking at rendered images.
- Never hand-edit `final/*.docx` or `final/*.xlsx`. Fix scripts/overrides and rebuild.

## Source
- PDF: `{{LANE}}/sources/{{FILE}}.pdf` ({{N}} pages), sha256 `{{SHA}}`.
- Probe result (from `probe.py`): text pages {{TEXT_PAGES}}; scanned pages {{SCAN_PAGES}};

## Rules (most important first)
1. **Keep the source's typos and wrong numbers.** Do not correct spelling, wrong totals, wrong page numbers in
   the contents list, or odd spacing. Transcribe what is printed, then list it under "source oddities".
2. **Do not guess.** If a word cannot be read, write `[?best guess]` (e.g. `[?สมบัติ]`). A signature is `[ลายมือชื่อ]`.
   A decoration or photo you do not reproduce: say so in your hand-back.
3. Digits: Arabic 0-9 always, even if the source prints Thai digits.
4. Scanned pages: render with PyMuPDF at 200 dpi into `work/scan-png/`, read the image, and write the page into
   `work/overrides/manual_pages.json` (block schema: `references/data-contract.md`). Never use the text layer of a
   scanned page (it is OCR garbage). Chart/org-chart pages: transcribe by eye and mark them for teacher review.
5. Do not add empty lines or spaces at wrapped line ends. A wrapped line joins the next one with a space only if the
   PDF line itself ended with a space.
6. Run the pipeline, read `work/REPORT-conversion.md`, and fix what is yours to fix (TODO pages, CHECK pages,
   Thai digits, wrap problems). Sum FAILs are source discrepancies: do not "fix" them, list them.

## Do this
1. `python3 ~/.codex/skills/thai-pdf-to-docx/scripts/pipeline.py --lane {{LANE}} sources/{{FILE}}.pdf --name {{NAME}} --title "{{TITLE}}" --render`
2. Look at the generic result first. Add `overrides/` entries (manual pages, table hooks, `config.json` markers such as
   book-specific list headings) only where the report or the page image shows a real problem. Re-run the pipeline.
3. Compare each overridden page and each table page against a 100 dpi render of the PDF page. Fix and re-run.

## Deliverables
- `final/{{NAME}}.docx` : 1 PDF page = 1 Word page; landscape pages as their own section; real tables with merges.
- `final/{{NAME}}-tables.xlsx` : sheets `index`, `T01...`, `text`.
- `work/REPORT-conversion.md` (generated by qa.py, do not write by hand) and `work/overrides/` (so the reviewer can re-run).

Files created; page and table counts; render page count; QA: pages not OK, sum FAILs (list them), wrap problems;
every `[?...]`; source oddities noticed; pages you transcribed by eye; what you could not do; what you want the
reviewer to look at first. Do not claim a check you did not run.
